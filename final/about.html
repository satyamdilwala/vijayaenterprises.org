About Vijaya Enterprises

Pioneering Indenting Agent and Exporter/Importer of Agricultural Commodities since 1995.

Our Legacy

Connecting Agricultural Producers with Global Buyers

Established in 1995, Vijaya Enterprises has grown into one of India's most trusted trade indenting and sourcing agencies. Under the visionary leadership of Mr. Tejas Thakkar, we bridge agricultural producers, wet milling processors, and feed manufacturers across international borders.

Our core expertise lies in sourcing Yellow Corn, Maize Gluten Meal (60%), Soya Meal, DORB, Wheat, Starches, Liquid Glucose, and dried fruits. We ensure absolute transparency, rigorous quality testing, and robust logistics from port to plant.
