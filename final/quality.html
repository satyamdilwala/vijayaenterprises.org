Our Quality Commitment

At Vijaya Enterprises, quality is non-negotiable. Every metric ton of Yellow Corn, Maize Gluten Meal, DORB, or Starch sourced through our trading desk undergoes standardized laboratory evaluation before shipment. We strictly adhere to FSSAI norms, ISO guidelines, and buyer-specific contract specifications.

4-Stage Quality Verification Process

Stage 01

Farm Sourcing

Direct audit of moisture content and kernel integrity at agricultural origin points.

Stage 02

Lab Analysis

Independent laboratory testing for Crude Protein, Moisture, Ash, Fiber, and Silica.

Stage 03

Packaging Audit

Checking bag stitching, inner liners, weight calibration, and export labeling.

Stage 04

Pre-Shipment Inspection

Final container sealing inspection and Certificate of Analysis issuance.
