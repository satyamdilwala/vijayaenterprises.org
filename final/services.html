Trade Services & Solutions

Comprehensive indenting, bulk sourcing, quality verification, & international logistics.

Trade Indenting & Sourcing

Acting as an authoritative indenting bridge between certified agricultural producers and international buyers, locking spot market quotes and contracts with complete transparency.

Quality Inspection & COA

Pre-shipment sampling and third-party laboratory testing for moisture, crude protein, fiber, fat, and AFLA toxin levels to ensure adherence to export standards.

End-to-End Logistics

Freight forwarding, container booking, customs documentation, phytosanitary clearance, and door-step transport execution for seamless international transit.

Customized Packaging

Flexible packaging solutions tailored to buyer requirements including 25kg PP bags, 50kg bags, bulk HDPE liner bags, and 1,000kg jumbo totes.

Market Intelligence

Regular crop yield forecasts, pricing trend alerts, and seasonal harvest insights to help clients optimize their purchasing timing and cost efficiency.

Export Documentation

Managing Certificate of Origin, Bill of Lading, Fumigation Certificates, Phytosanitary approvals, and LC compliance without administrative friction.
